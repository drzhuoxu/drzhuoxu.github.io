Zhuo Xu

Zhuo Xu is a co-founder of General Intelligent Machines (GIM) and a professor at UT Austin directing the Machine Intelligence Group (MIG). He had been a research scientist at Google DeepMind since earning his Ph.D. from UC Berkeley, advised by Prof. Masayoshi Tomizuka, and his B.E. at Tsinghua University.
